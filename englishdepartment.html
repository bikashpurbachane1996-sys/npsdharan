Faculty of Languages & Literature

Department of English

Empowering young minds through linguistic excellence, literary depth, persuasive oratory, and global communicative proficiency.

Faculty Teachers — Department of English, NPS Dharan
Nurturing Expression

Inspiring Articulacy & Literary Passion

The English Department at Nepal Police School Dharan is committed to fostering critical reading, imaginative writing, public speaking, and communicative fluency. Through interactive debates, drama, Model United Nations, and rigorous syllabus instruction, our teaching faculty equips students to excel in higher studies and global professional arenas.

Comprehensive Literary & Communicative Curriculum
Oratory, Debate, & Model UN Mentorship
Proven Track Record in Board Examinations
