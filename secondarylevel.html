Secondary Level (Grade 9 - 10)

Excellence in Secondary Education

The Secondary Level (Grades 9 and 10) is the pinnacle of school education where students prepare intensively for the Secondary Education Examination (SEE) under the guidance of veteran faculty members.

Nepal Police School Dharan emphasizes conceptual clarity, regular test series, practical laboratory hours, career counseling, and uncompromising discipline to ensure every student achieves top grades and builds a strong platform for +2 studies.
