Cultivating Responsible Citizens

Civic Awareness & Heritage Fieldwork

The Social Studies Department connects theoretical knowledge with real-world community engagement. Through mock youth parliaments, regional historical expeditions across Province 1, environmental survey camps, and national constitution workshops, our students develop deep patriotism and critical global perspectives.
