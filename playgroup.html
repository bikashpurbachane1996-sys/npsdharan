Playgroup Program

A joyful, safe, and nurturing early learning environment designed to spark curiosity and build foundational social skills for our youngest learners.

Welcome to Our Early Learning Center

At Nepal Police School Dharan, our Playgroup program serves as a child's very first step into structured schooling. We focus on transforming the transition from home to school into an exciting, comforting, and engaging adventure.

Through sensory play, interactive storytelling, music, and guided creative expression, our experienced early childhood educators ensure that toddlers develop confidence, basic motor coordination, and a natural love for learning.

Our Playgroup Approach

Core developmental dimensions tailored specifically for toddlers and early learners.

Play-Way Learning

Learning concepts are introduced through educational toys, puzzles, blocks, and guided physical games that stimulate cognitive growth naturally without pressure.

Social & Emotional Growth

Children learn how to share space, interact gently with peers, express their emotions properly, and build early trust with caring mentors outside their homes.

Creative Expression

Finger painting, clay molding, rhythm sessions, and storytelling help unlock imagination, fine motor control, and linguistic confidence from an early age.
