Department of Science

Fostering scientific inquiry, empirical experimentation, and analytical thinking across Physics, Chemistry, and Biology to prepare future innovators.

Next-Generation Science Laboratories

At Nepal Police School Dharan, scientific theories come to life. Our specialized Physics, Chemistry, and Biology laboratories provide state-of-the-art apparatus, digital sensors, and safety infrastructure enabling students from middle school through Grade 12 (+2 Science) to master rigorous experimentation.
