Our Glorious History & Heritage

Tracing the remarkable journey of Nepal Police School from its inception to becoming a pillar of academic and disciplinary excellence.

Quick Overview of School History

Founded in 2040 BS by the then IGP Late D. B. Lama

Nepal Police School is a not-for-profit educational institution run by the Nepal Police organization under the patronage of the Nepal Police Educational Trust. Popularly acronymed as NPS and affectionately known as N'cops/D'cops, the school was established in 2040 BS by the then IGP Late D. B. Lama.

The primary objective was to impart quality education to the children of police personnel who, on the course of their duty of serving the nation, remain away from their families. Later, the school doors were opened to the general public as well. With its long glorious history, the institution has established itself as a premier educational landmark in the nation.

Surrounded by lush greenery and serene environments, the school provides an inspiring learning atmosphere far from the hustle, bustle, and pollution of urban city life, fostering wholesome growth and character development for every student.
