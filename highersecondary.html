NEB Affiliated +2 Program (Grades 11 & 12)

Management Stream with Computer Science

Empowering energetic young leaders with modern business acumen, financial literacy, and cutting-edge computer science skills for the digital economy.

</> Business Meets Technology

Shaping Future Entrepreneurs & Tech Innovators

Our +2 Management program integrated with Computer Science is tailored specifically for ambitious mid-teens who want the best of both worlds: robust foundational training in accounting, economics, and business studies, paired with practical programming, database management, and digital problem-solving.

With disciplined hostel environments, experienced faculty, and rigorous mentorship, we prepare students for top-tier university degrees in BBA, BIM, BCA, BIT, and BSc.CSIT.

Scholarship & Financial Aid Programs

Rewarding academic excellence and supporting deserving students in their +2 journey.

Merit Scholarships

Full and partial tuition fee waivers awarded to top-ranking SEE graduates based on entrance test scores and board results.

Police Welfare Quota

Special concession quotas and welfare scholarships designated for children of serving and retired Nepal Police personnel.

Need-Based Assistance

Financial support and community assistance grants for economically marginalized yet academically brilliant students.
