Purbiya Jyoti Book (पूर्बिय ज्योति)

Our flagship annual publication showcases the incredible literary talent and artistic imagination of Nepal Police School students. Inside, you will find captivating short stories, thought-provoking novels, funny student jokes, brilliant sketches and drawings, teacher reflections, motivational messages, and official group photographs of every class, department, and police personnel squad.

What's Inside the Magazine?

A glimpse into the diverse creative sections curated by our students and faculty.

Stories & Novels

Imaginative short stories, serialized novel chapters, and creative essays written by budding student authors.

Drawings & Sketches

Vibrant artwork, pencil sketches, nature paintings, and comic illustrations created by our talented student artists.

Teacher & Staff Quotes

Inspirational messages, guidance notes, and motivational thoughts shared by teachers and police commanders.

Class & Staff Portfolios

Official group photographs of every class section, academic departments, and the dedicated police personnel squad.
