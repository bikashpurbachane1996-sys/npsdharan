Co-Curricular & Extra-Curricular Excellence

ECA & CCA Activities

Nurturing physical fitness, teamwork, leadership, and artistic expression through our vibrant Friday co-curricular and sports events.

Every Friday Event Showcase

At Nepal Police School Dharan, every Friday afternoon is dedicated entirely to Extra-Curricular (ECA) and Co-Curricular (CCA) events. Students engage in inter-house football and basketball tournaments, elocution contests, spelling bees, cultural folk dances, karate drills, and science exhibitions, ensuring holistic development beyond the classroom.
