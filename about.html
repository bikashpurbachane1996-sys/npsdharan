About Nepal Police School Dharan

Nurturing future leaders through educational excellence, physical discipline, and patriotic service to the nation.

Fostering Leadership & Moral Character

Nepal Police School Dharan is dedicated to delivering holistic, forward-looking education under the aegis of the Nepal Police Educational Trust. Our comprehensive pedagogical approach seamlessly combines rigorous modern academic learning with character building and discipline.

We provide an inclusive and structured learning environment that prepares students to meet global academic standards while cultivating profound ethical values and societal responsibility.

Foundational Pillars

Guiding principles that direct our institution toward continuous excellence.

Our Mission

To empower students with superior academic competence, creative intellect, and leadership capabilities while inculcating an unwavering sense of moral rectitude and social accountability.

Our Vision

To be an exceptional center of learning and character development, recognized nationally for producing disciplined, visionary, and patriotic citizens equipped to thrive in an evolving world.

Our Goals

To maintain high academic standards, promote sports and ECA/CCA participation, ensure state-of-the-art infrastructure, and nurture the holistic growth of every child under our care.

Our Core Motto: DDD

At Nepal Police School Dharan, our institutional discipline and conduct are guided by the three sacred pillars:

Discipline
Dignity
Decorum
