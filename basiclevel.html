🌟 Basic Level (Grade 1 - 5) 🌟

Welcome to our vibrant primary section where young minds discover the joy of learning, creativity, and foundational confidence!

Growing & Learning Together!

Our Basic Level program (Grades 1 through 5) is carefully crafted to build strong fundamental skills in literacy, numeracy, science, and social values. We believe every child is unique and learns best in a cheerful, encouraging, and safe environment.

With interactive smart classrooms, friendly teachers, and activity-based lessons, our students enjoy coming to school every day eager to explore new concepts and make wonderful friends.

What Makes Learning Fun Here?

Exciting subjects and activities designed specially for growing kids.

Fun Literacy & Languages

Engaging storytelling, phonics, creative writing, and bilingual expression in Nepali and English to build strong communication skills.

Exciting Math Magic

Exploring numbers, shapes, and puzzles through practical counting tools and interactive math games that make numbers fun.

Little Scientists

Safe, hands-on science experiments and nature walks to spark curiosity about plants, animals, weather, and the world around us.

Art & Craft Studio

Unleashing creativity through drawing, coloring, origami, clay work, and crafts that help develop fine motor skills.

Sports & Playtime

Active outdoor games, mini-footballs, races, and team activities to keep little bodies healthy, energetic, and coordinated.

Music & Dance

Joyful cultural dance sessions, school choir, and rhythmic movements that foster rhythm, teamwork, and cultural appreciation.
