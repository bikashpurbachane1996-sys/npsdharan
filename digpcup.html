About the Championship

The Koshi Province Interschool DIGP Running Football Tournament is our marquee annual athletic event, bringing together top secondary schools across the entire province for an epic week of competitive football.

Hosted on our professional school grounds in Dharan, the tournament champions teamwork, discipline, and athletic excellence in honor of police educational leadership.

Schedule: Poush 03 - Poush 11, 2083
