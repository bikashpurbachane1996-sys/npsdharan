Message From The Chairperson

Guiding our institution towards higher horizons of academic distinction, moral resilience, and national duty.

SSP Gyanendra Prasad Fuyal
Chairperson (Adhyakshya)
School Management Committee
  • Nepal Police School Dharan
  • Senior Superintendent of Police
  • Dharan-13, Sunsari, Nepal
  • chairperson@npsdharan.edu.np

Empowering Minds, Inspiring Character, and Serving the Nation

“Education is not merely the acquisition of knowledge; it is the building of character, the conditioning of discipline, and the pursuit of selfless service to humanity.”

It is with immense honor and pride that I welcome you to Nepal Police School Dharan. Established under the visionary umbrella of the Nepal Police Educational Trust, our institution remains steadfastly committed to imparting wholesome, world-class education rooted in the sacred values of discipline, integrity, and patriotism.

In an era driven by rapid technological advancements and evolving global standards, education must transcend traditional textbook memorization. At NPS Dharan, our objective is to cultivate critical inquiry, moral courage, and emotional resilience within every young boy and girl who enters our gates. We take pride in honoring our foundational motto DDD: Discipline, Dignity, and Decorum, weaving these principles seamlessly into our academic curricula, residential routines, and athletic endeavors.

Our dedicated faculty members, pastoral wardens, and administrative staff work tirelessly as a cohesive unit to ensure that our campus serves as a nurturing second home. Whether in competitive national examinations, inter-school football trophies, scientific contests, or humanitarian initiatives, our students have continuously brought laurels to the nation and the Nepal Police fraternity.

Discipline & Honor

Inculcating self-discipline, ethics, and patriotic fervor.

Academic Rigor

Fostering scientific innovation and intellectual brilliance.

Holistic Growth

Nurturing physical health, creativity, and empathy.

To our dear parents and guardians, I express my heartfelt gratitude for your enduring trust in our institution. We consider education to be a three-way partnership between the student, the school, and the family. Together, we shall continue to sculpt responsible citizens and visionary leaders capable of steering our country towards a brighter and more harmonious future.

May Lord Pashupatinath bless our institution, our beloved students, and all members of the Nepal Police School family.

SSP Gyanendra Prasad Fuyal

Chairperson, School Management Committee
