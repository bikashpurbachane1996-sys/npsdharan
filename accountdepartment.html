Fiscal Integrity & Transparency

Professional Bookkeeping & Financial Simulation

The Accountancy Department at Nepal Police School Dharan bridges theoretical accounting principles with practical corporate accounting. Utilizing computerized accounting software (Tally/ERP), automated ledger simulations, and rigorous auditing projects, our students acquire the analytical acumen required for modern commerce and management.
