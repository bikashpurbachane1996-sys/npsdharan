Our School Facilities

Providing an enriching, safe, and modern environment where academic excellence, sportsmanship, and personal growth thrive together.

Accommodation

Hostel (Residential Facility)

Our school is a fully residential institution proviving hostel facility for students in Grades 4–10 and for Grades 11–12 in the Management stream. The hostel environment is designed to feel like a second home, offering warmth, care, and a supportive atmosphere. A dedicated pastoral management team—including the Vice Principal (Pastoral), wardens (Guru/Guruma), and residential medical attendants—ensures holistic well-being, disciplined routines, and a profound sense of community.

Spacious Dormitories Medical Facility 24/7 Pastoral Care Dedicated Prep Hours(Morning and Evening Classes)
Transit & Mobility

Transportation Services

To facilitate convenient, safe, and punctual commuting for day-boarders, day scholars, and off-campus academic excursions, the school operates a well-managed fleet of buses covering key routes across Dharan, and surrounding vicinities. Every vehicle is staffed with experienced drivers and attendants adhering strictly to road safety protocols.

Safe & Punctual Routes Dharan Coverage Trained Transport Staff
Nutrition & Dining

Mess & Hygienic Canteen

Physical vigor and academic alertness require wholesome nourishment. Our expansive dining hall serves fresh, balanced, and nutritious meals prepared under stringent standards of hygiene and cleanliness. Weekly menus are structured by dietary considerations, providing our boarders and staff with wholesome vegetarian and non-vegetarian selections.

Balanced Diet Menu Modern Steam Kitchen High Hygiene Standards
Athletics & Fitness

Sports & Physical Infrastructure

True to our police heritage, physical education and athletic endurance form a core pillar of student life. We boast international standard football grounds, basketball courts, volleyball arenas, athletic tracks, table tennis halls, and lawn tennis facilities. Students train daily under certified sports coaches and police PT instructors.

Full-size Football Ground Multi-Sport Courts Police Drill & PT
Eco-Friendly Campus

Clean, Green & Safe Environment

Nestled in the lush hills of Dharan-13, Sunsari, our sprawling campus offers an idyllic, pollution-free learning sanctuary. Shaded groves, manicured gardens, wide-open courtyards, and pure mountain air offer an inspiring escape from urban noise, encouraging concentration, mental health, and physical vitality.

Zero-Pollution Zone Purified RO Drinking Water Resident Infirmary
Knowledge Center

Modern Resource Library

Our well-stocked library serves as the intellectual heart of the school. Housing thousands of titles ranging from curriculum textbooks, encyclopedias, reference guides, and competitive exam materials to fiction, periodicals, and academic journals. Quiet study cubicles inspire students to develop lifelong reading and research habits.

Extensive Book Collection National Periodicals Quiet Self-Study Halls
Technology & Computing

Hi-Tech Computer Laboratory

Equipped with modern desktop terminals, high-speed optical fiber internet, and interactive multimedia teaching aids, our computer laboratory provides practical IT education from early grades through senior secondary levels. Students master programming concepts, software skills, and digital literacy under expert guidance.

1:1 Student Computer Ratio Dedicated High-Speed Fiber Programming & ICT Labs
Safety & Protection

24/7 CCTV Surveillance & Police Security

Student security and campus safety are paramount. The entire academic block, hostels, perimeter gates, and grounds are covered under round-the-clock high-definition CCTV monitoring. In addition, the campus is guarded 24 hours a day by trained Nepal Police security personnel, guaranteeing peace of mind to parents.

Full HD Perimeter Coverage Nepal Police Gate Patrol Controlled Visitor Access
